About this simulation

A double pendulum: two point masses on massless rigid rods, swinging in a vertical plane. It is chaotic -- two runs that start almost identically soon look completely different -- so the path of the outer mass is unpredictable, while the total energy stays constant.

Simulation

How it's simulated

  • The state is just the two rod angles and their angular velocities. They're integrated in a SOFA Controller each animation step with classical Runge-Kutta (RK4), using the Euler-Lagrange equations of motion. The mass positions follow exactly from the angles, so the rods can't stretch.
  • The energy drift readout is the relative change in total energy since the start: the accuracy check for a chaotic system, where no single trajectory can be "right".
  • SOFA runs headless in the container; the browser draws the scene with three.js and the graphs with Chart.js, fed over a WebSocket.
  • Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. The view is yours alone; it doesn't affect anyone else watching.
